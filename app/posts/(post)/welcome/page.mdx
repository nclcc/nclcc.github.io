# Why this site exists

LinkedIn rewards short, polished bullet points. Most of the work that goes into a model — the
reason a particular method was chosen, the assumption that *almost* broke it in production, the
analytical detour that turned out to be the actual insight — doesn't survive that compression.

This site is the long-form record. Six engagements are written up under
[Experience](/#experience); each one includes the methods that worked, the methods that quietly
didn't, and the reasoning behind the choice.

## The thesis

Most quantitative tooling is built for the convenient case: stationarity, finite variance,
independent shocks. The systems I build live in the inconvenient case — heavy tails, regime
shifts, structural breaks, rare events that disproportionately drive outcomes.

That's a methodological choice, not a marketing one. It's why the philosophy section is on the
homepage and not buried in a footer. Six principles, six honest constraints; you can read them
[here](/#philosophy).

## What to expect

I'll post here irregularly — short notes on:

- Specific methods (fractal estimators, EVT tail diagnostics, regime classifiers) and where
  they break
- The recurring places where standard quantitative tooling silently fails on real data
- Occasional retrospectives on engagements once they've shipped and embargoes lift
- Things I'm reading that I think are good

No SEO, no posting cadence, no list-building. If a piece of writing is useful, it goes up.

If something here resonates — for collaboration, hiring, or argument — the comments are open
below, or my [contact](/#contact) is on the homepage.
